FarmSight 360 — Agricultural Management Sample App

A streamlined mock of FarmSight 360 backed by PostgreSQL and authenticated through a dedicated Keycloak realm (farm360) and OIDC client (farm360-web).

🌾
Farm & Crop Lifecycle

Track farm plots, crop varieties, acreage, growth stages, and expected harvest yields.

👷
Workforce & Daily Attendance

Manage farm workers, daily wage rates, and mark real-time muster roll attendance.

🚜
Service Provider Marketplace

Farmers book machinery & drone spraying; Service Providers list equipment and fulfill bookings.

🛡️ Administrator Access (Keycloak Webpage)

Administrators manage users, reset login passwords, assign roles (farmer, service_provider, admin), and disable accounts directly in the official Keycloak Admin Console:
